## When it occurs

The handler ran, but its response exceeded `MaxResponseBytes` or could not be buffered. The default limit is 1 MiB. Execution could neither store nor return the response. A business effect may already have happened.

## Client handling

Do not repeat the operation automatically. Use `operation_ref` with an operator to establish the outcome. Operators should keep responses under the limit or raise it. A new key is safe only when `safe_new_key` is true.

## How to fix

Bound the handler's non-streaming response and review MaxResponseBytes together with the journal's MaxResultBytes. Inspect the operation before retrying; response overflow does not prove an absent effect.
