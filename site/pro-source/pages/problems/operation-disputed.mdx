## When it occurs

Recorded results disagree about whether the operation applied. For example, a provider later reported success for an operation recorded as not applied. Execution marks a duplicate risk and never replays such an operation.

## Client handling

Do not retry automatically, and do not use a new key. Report `operation_ref` to an authorized operator, who must resolve the dispute.

## Example response

The package may omit diagnostic text or return it empty. Operation references do not grant access. Custom handlers can replace the body.

## How to fix

Stop automatic business retries and inspect contradictory provider and operator records. Preserve the original key and reference. Resolve the dispute through the authorized recovery path; duplicate risk forbids a new key.
