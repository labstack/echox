## When it occurs

An operator closed the operation as abandoned, or attested without complete proof that it was not applied. The decision is an attestation, not proof that no effect happened.

## Client handling

Attestation is not permission to retry. Do not retry automatically, and follow the operator's instructions. A new key is safe only when `safe_new_key` is true.

## Example response

The package may omit diagnostic text or return it empty. Operation references do not grant access. Custom handlers can replace the body.

## How to fix

Keep the abandoned operation and provider records available to authorized operators. Abandonment does not prove that no send applied or grant a fresh key. Follow the recorded recovery decision before any further effect.
