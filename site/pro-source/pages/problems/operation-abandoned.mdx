## When it occurs

An operator closed the operation as abandoned, or declared it not applied without full proof. This is the operator's statement; the effect may still have happened.

## Client handling

The operator's decision does not allow a retry. Do not retry automatically, and follow the operator's instructions. A new key is safe only when `safe_new_key` is true.

## Server correction

Keep the operation and provider records available. Abandoning an operation does not prove that no send happened or allow a new key. Follow the recorded recovery decision.
