## When it occurs

A panel callback timed out or the request was cancelled. `Config.Timeout` covers the entire request, including middleware. It defaults to ten seconds and accepts one millisecond through one minute.

## Client handling

Retry later, or narrow the request, for example with a smaller `limit` or a shorter period. Operators should check the backend's latency.
