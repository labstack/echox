{/* Diataxis: how-to */}

Choose a task for the part of your application you are adding:

- [Auth](/pro/guides/auth/): API keys and bearer tokens.
- [Tenant](/pro/guides/tenant/): tenant selection and resource permissions.
- [Quota](/pro/guides/quota/): plans, limits and credits.
- [Webhook](/pro/guides/webhook/): signed receivers and durable outgoing delivery.
- [Audit](/pro/guides/audit/): business events, search and export.
- [Execution](/pro/guides/execution/): idempotency keys and recovery from uncertain sends.
- [Admin](/pro/guides/admin/): operator access and panel actions.

[Getting started](/pro/getting-started/) runs authentication, tenants, quotas and signed delivery together.
