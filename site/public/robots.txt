# Echo's documentation is MIT-licensed and we want it read, indexed, and
# cited — by search engines, by AI answer engines, and by the coding agents
# developers now use to write Echo code. The policy is allow-everything.

# The per-agent groups below are redundant against `User-agent: *` today.
# They are here so the intent is recorded rather than inferred, and so a
# future change to a crawler's or a CDN's defaults does not silently opt
# Echo out. Blocking retrieval and agent crawlers is what removes a site
# from AI answers; blocking training crawlers does not affect citations.
